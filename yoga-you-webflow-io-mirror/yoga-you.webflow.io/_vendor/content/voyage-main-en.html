Itinerary · 12 days

Each day has its own landscape. Flights are still to be confirmed; the ground programme is that of the circuit.

Algiers

Day 1 · Thursday — Marseille → Algiers

Meet at 7:30 at Marseille for Air Algérie AH1023 (10:30). Arrive Algiers around 12:30, visa formalities, transfer to Sofitel Hamma. Afternoon in the Jardin d’Essai. Night at the hotel.

Day 2 · Friday — Algiers and flight to Djanet

Breakfast, left luggage, visit of Algiers the White: Grande Poste, Place des Martyrs, Casbah. Transfer to the domestic airport. Flight AH6312 at 23:00 to Djanet.

Essendilène camel trek

Day 3 · Saturday — Djanet, stone forest

Arrive Djanet around 1:15, welcome by Tuareg guides, first night at camp. Morning: market (chèches), park formalities, meet the camels. Trek through the stone forest, bivouac at Adayig.

Day 4 · Sunday — Ineloukou & Tilalen wadis

Descent into the Ineloukou wadi, red dunes, then Tilalen at the foot of the cliffs. Rock paintings. Dinner and night in bivouac.

Day 5 · Monday — Tikobaouine

Crossing a sea of dunes toward Tikobaouine: wind-carved stone arch, guelta. Bivouac on site.

Day 6 · Tuesday — Essendilène canyon

Vast canyon with sheer walls, gradual descent toward Essendilène. Bivouac in this setting.

Day 7 · Wednesday — Guelta and back to Djanet

Essendilène guelta, farewell to the camels, lunch, 4×4 transfer to Djanet. Tegharghart camp: showers, dinner, night at camp.

Tadrat Rouge

Day 8 · Thursday — El Berj & Moulennaga

4×4 to El Berj, lunch in the wild, yellow dunes of Moulennaga. Night in bivouac among the dunes.

Day 9 — Indjaren wadi & Timerzouga

Morning walk in the Moulennaga dunes, meet the vehicles in the Indjaren wadi, then Timerzouga. Night in bivouac.

Day 10 · Sunday — Adjalati

Visit the “World Cup” rock formation, continue to Adjalati. Bivouac.

Day 11 · Monday — Crying Cow & Djanet

Crying Cow rock engraving, return to Djanet, Tuareg market, musical evening. Night transfer to the airport.

Day 12 · Tuesday — Djanet → Algiers → Marseille

Flight AH6232 around 4:15, Algiers 6:30, then international AH1020 (13:40) arriving Marseille around 15:10. Times indicative, to be confirmed.
