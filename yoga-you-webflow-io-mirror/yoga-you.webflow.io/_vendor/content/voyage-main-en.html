Three chapters, one desert

The journey starts in Algiers, continues as a camel trek with Tuareg cameleers toward Essendilène, then opens onto the Tadrat Rouge by 4×4. 15 to 20 km a day on the méharée, 8 to 10 km on the 4×4 days. A living pace, never a race.
1

Days 1 and 2

Algiers the White

Arrival from Marseille, a night at the Sofitel Hamma, a walk in the Jardin d’Essai, then the Casbah, Grande Poste and Place des Martyrs. Evening flight to Djanet.
Air Algérie Marseille → Algiers
1 night at Sofitel Hamma, breakfast included
Night flight to Djanet
2

Days 3 to 7

Camel trek to Essendilène

Welcome by Tamasheq guides, Djanet market for chèches, then departure with the camels: stone forest, wadis, rock paintings, Tikobaouine arch, canyon and Essendilène guelta.
15 to 20 km a day, 4 hours morning, 2 hours afternoon
Bivouacs, 4×4 team for logistics
Return to Djanet, Tegharghart camp
3

Days 8 to 12

Tadrat Rouge by 4×4

Moulennaga dunes, Indjaren wadi, the “World Cup” rock, the Crying Cow engraving, Djanet market and a musical evening before the return flight to Marseille via Algiers.
Walks of 8 to 10 km a day
Landmark sites of the cultural park, a UNESCO World Heritage site
Return Djanet → Algiers → Marseille
